## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic hero illustration for the approved PokéMMO Tiquicia Edition website. Create a polished wide landscape in authentic detailed 2D pixel art, inspired by the Caribbean lowlands of Costa Rica and the area of Pococí. A coherent small tropical village, modest colorful houses with tin roofs, a central park, lush vegetation and palms, a winding turquoise river and a little bridge, distant rainforest mountains and warm cloud-filled sky. Welcoming adventurous afternoon atmosphere. Deep blue, tropical greens, muted teal, warm cream light. Wide cinematic horizontal composition: leave the LEFT THIRD visually calm and darker with soft forest silhouettes, because the webpage places its title and button there; place the village, river and most beautiful details in the CENTER AND RIGHT. Pixel clusters and deliberate limited color shading, tasteful retro monster-training RPG art. No text, no logo, no watermark, no user interface, no screenshots, no characters or Pokémon. This is original promotional scenery, not a reproduction of the actual playable map.
